# GoMate: Go AI & Game Review

GoMate: Go AI & Game Review is for people who want to play Go against a computer opponent that fits their level, and then understand the game afterwards. It runs on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account to create and your games stay on your device.

## An opponent that isn't too strong or too weak

Many Go apps drop a newcomer in front of an opponent far above their level. In GoMate you choose your opponent from 16 levels, from Beginner Coach up to Super AI and Max AI, and you can set handicap stones and komi before each game. The same app can follow you from your first games to a ranked level.

Beginner Coach is the gentlest level. Its playing strength is the lowest, and on each move it chooses the move that keeps the position closest to even. That way a beginner is neither overwhelmed nor handed an easy win. Beginner Coach is the one paid opponent level, unlocked either with a one-time purchase or with a yearly subscription. The other 15 levels and every feature in the app are free, with no ads and no limit on the number of games.

## Play with hints only when you want them

A coach switch sits inside the game. When it is off, you get a clean board and nothing else. When it is on, you see a win-rate bar and suggested points, labelled with the shape name where the app can recognise one. If it can't recognise a shape, it marks the point and does not invent a name.

You can take back a move, pass, and check the territory at any point during the game. At the end, the score is shown either as area counting or as territory counting, whichever you prefer.

## Review the game once it's over

Save a finished game and open it later to step forward and back through every move. Review shows a win-rate curve for the whole game, with good moves and questionable moves marked on the board and on the curve. You can also see how much territory each side holds, from either Black's or White's point of view.

You can import an SGF file from elsewhere and have it analysed the same way. If the file has problems, the app lists them and asks whether you want to continue.

## Your games stay on your device

The AI runs on the device itself. GoMate has no account and no online play, and your saved games are stored only on your device. Purchases, if you make one, are handled by the App Store.

## What it doesn't do

GoMate does not offer online matches against other people, cloud sync, or a library of Go problems.
